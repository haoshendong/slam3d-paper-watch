方法本质拆解 · What actually changed

训练

TokenMatch 使用 ViT-Base backbone 和 masked auto-encoding pretraining，再在 BeCoS partial-to-partial correspondence 上训练统一模型。官方原文明确指出测试 full shapes 不需要 retraining 或 fine-tuning。

约束

输入约束来自 mesh connectivity、曲率/谱几何信号和 geodesic neighborhoods，而不是外部模板语义。损失同时约束 Chamfer reconstruction、局部关系、overlap 与 functional map。

推理

推理时对输入 mesh 做 curvature-guided tokenisation，运行 self/cross attention 并预测 overlap 与 correspondence。它是 sub-second feed-forward 模型，不是在每一对形状上运行迭代式全局优化。

机制

TokenMatch 把不规则 mesh 的几何细节压缩为有重叠 token，再用跨形状交互恢复 dense relation。适度 overlap 让局部 token 的信息连续，但噪声和极小重叠会破坏这一传递。

输入 mesh 与曲率/谱信号，改变 tokenisation 和跨形状交互位置，再输出 dense correspondence/functional map。

01

动机与问题Motivation

动机与问题

部分形状、非等距变形和缺少可靠模板会破坏手工 descriptor 或生成式 functional map 方法。研究问题是能否用统一 transformer 接口在不重新训练的情况下恢复重叠区域、对应关系和 functional map。

02

方法Method

方法总览

方法先用 curvature-guided farthest point sampling 选 token center，再以 geodesic influence radius 建立软重叠 token。Transformer 经过 masked auto-encoding pretraining、cross-shape feature interaction、overlap prediction 和 functional map generation。

关键技术细节

顶点信号为 α‖H(i)‖₂+(1−α)E(i)，其中 H 是 mean curvature、E 是 spectral signal；token overlap 通过 geodesic distance soft assignment 实现。训练目标结合 Chamfer reconstruction、feature relation、overlap 与 functional-map 相关损失。

03

实验Experiments

实验设置

数据集包括 CP2P、PSMAL、BeCoS、FAUST、SCAPE 和 SHREC’19，指标包括 mean geodesic error 与 mean IoU。实现使用 ViT-Base，12 layers、hidden width 768、MLP dimension 3072、12 heads，g=256 tokens。

实验数字与比较

BeCoS 上 partial-to-partial 训练测试 mean IoU 为 65.25，partial-to-full 直接测试为 55.61，full-to-full 直接测试为 71.85。官方 Table 9 的 token overlap 消融和附录 noise robustness 表明中等 overlap 更稳定；具体逐项数值以 source.md 附录表核验。

消融/失败边界

去掉 cross-shape interaction 会削弱跨形状关系建模，改变 token overlap degree 和 token 数也会影响 GE/IoU；官方附录把这些列为独立消融。失败边界包括严重噪声、极小重叠、强非等距形变和曲率信号不能区分语义对应的形状。

原文摘要 · Abstract

TokenMatch 用曲率与谱几何信号把 mesh 自适应划分为带重叠的 patch tokens，再用 self/cross attention 学习 dense correspondence。它只在 BeCoS partial-to-partial 上训练，却能迁移到 full-to-full 和 partial-to-full matching。

While data-driven 3D shape correspondence estimation has recently seen substantial progress, robust matching under partial observations and strong non-isometric deformations remains challenging. Existing learning-based approaches often rely on hand-crafted descriptors or template-based representations, whereas recent generative models over functional maps suffer from high inference cost, limited interpretability, and poor generalisation to partial shapes. In response to these limitations, this paper introduces TokenMatch, a new transformer-based unified model for estimating 3D shape correspondences. Our feed-forward approach trained exclusively on BeCoS, a challenging non-isometric partial-to-partial shape-matching dataset, can generalise to matching full shapes without retraining or fine-tuning. TokenMatch uses self- and cross-attention mechanisms to efficiently learn patch-level and point-level relations as well as dense correspondences between shape pairs. Our core insight is that meshes can be adaptively tokenised into patches using shape curvature guidance, enabling effective learning of shape-specific geometric descriptors for correspondence estimation. We evaluate TokenMatch on standard benchmarks for partial and full shape matching, including CP2P, PSMAL, BeCoS, FAUST, SCAPE, and SHREC'19. Our method achieves consistently high performance, in most cases outperforming existing methods for partial and full shape matching in the mean geodesic error and intersection-over-union metrics, while also running faster at sub-second inference speeds.
